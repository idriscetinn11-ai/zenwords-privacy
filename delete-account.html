Delete Your ZenWords Account

If you would like to permanently delete your ZenWords account and all associated learning data, please contact us using the email below.

Email: support@zerensoftapp.com

Subject: Delete My ZenWords Account

After verifying ownership of the account, we will permanently delete:

  • Your account
  • Your learning progress
  • Your scores
  • Your known and review words
  • Your premium status
  • Your drop balance

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days.
